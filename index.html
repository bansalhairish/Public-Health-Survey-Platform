    Privacy controls in this platform

    • Anonymous surveys store no name and no personal details.
    • Respondents can only see published surveys, never results.
    • Results are hidden until at least 3 responses exist, so one person cannot be identified.
    • Dashboard, builder and export are available only to logged-in staff.
    • All data stays in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.
